Jithsur: Rules

Life and winning

  • You start at 25 life, and life is capped at 25.
  • "Remove damage from a player" means restoring life toward 25.
  • You win only when your opponent hits 0 life or runs out of cards to draw.

Deck

  • A deck has at least 50 cards, with no sideboard.
  • You can run up to 4 copies of a card.
  • Opening hand: draw 7, set aside any you don't want, draw that many, then shuffle the set-aside cards back in.

Turn order

  • First, all Resources go back to ready.
  • Then you draw, except on turn 1 unless it's multiplayer.
  • Then come Main 1, Combat, Main 2, and End.
  • There's no upkeep step, so timers like Unstable are written on the card.
  • The stack and priority work the same as in Magic.

Resources

  • Any card can go into the Resource Pool, one per turn, during your Main phase.
  • In the Pool a card is only a Resource, and you can use it the same turn it goes in.
  • Cards go into the Pool upside down so the element icon shows at the top.
  • Ready Resources sit to the right, and used ones slide to the left.
  • Element symbols in a cost must be paid with matching Resources, and 🔘 can be paid with any element.
  • When you use a dual Resource, you pick one of its elements.
  • 12 Aether Effects can turn the card they're attached to into a dual Resource.

Zones

  • The Aether is like Magic's graveyard. Played, discarded, or destroyed cards go there unless the text says otherwise.
  • Exile, also called remove from the game, means gone for good.
  • A card cast from the Aether is exiled once it resolves.

Speed and card types

  • Creatures, Characters, and Continuous Magics are cast only on your own turn, in a Main phase, unless the text says otherwise.
  • Magic Spells and Aether Effects can be cast at any time unless the text says otherwise.
  • Continuous Magic covers what artifacts and enchantments do in Magic.

Characters

  • You can control only one Character of each name. If a second one comes in, you choose which to keep.
  • Characters are generally Rare and meant to be strong.

Combat

  • Damage stays on a card until something removes it, and a card with 1 or more damage is Wounded.
  • Damage goes both ways when attacking and when blocked.
  • There's no tapping, so cards can attack on your turn and block on theirs.
  • Attacks go at the player by default.
  • Attacks Directly lets an attacker pick a Character or Creature instead. That target is locked into combat, and blockers can still join.

Keywords

  • Rush lets a card attack the turn it comes in, like Haste in Magic.
  • Outmaneuver means the attacker deals combat damage and takes none back.
  • Unstable works like Fading, and the card says when its counters come off.
  • Guardian is the blocker keyword, and the word Defender is never used.
  • Blinking re-triggers "enters the Battlefield" abilities.
  • Effects that grant something to a card last until end of turn.

Elements

  • The elements are Fire, Earth, Wind, Water, Radiant, Decay, and Neutral.
  • Neutral cards have cheaper, all-🔘 costs, can only make 🔘 as Resources, and can't take Aether Effects.

Aether Effects

  • You attach Aether Effects to cards while building your deck: one per card, matching the card's element, and up to 4 copies of each.
  • An Aether Effect goes to the Aether with its card, and you can cast it from there. It's exiled once it resolves.
  • Spellbound means you cast the attached Aether Effect while its card is still alive, and the card is then exiled when it dies.
  • Aether Effects work the same online and on paper.
